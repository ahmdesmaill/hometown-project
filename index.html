Visit Warsaw

Discover history and culture in this vibrant city on the Vistula river.

Top three activities to do at Warsaw

Explore the Royal Castle & Old Town

Step back in time through cobblestone streets and a beautifully rebuilt royal palace.

Visit the Palace of Culture & Science

Take an elevator to the observation deck for panoramic views of the city skyline.

Relax in Royal Łazienki Park

Listen to a Chopin concert or watch the peacocks roam near the Palace on the Isle.
